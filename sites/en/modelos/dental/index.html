FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with most PPO plans and happy to file out-of-network claims too. Send us your plan when you book and we'll confirm your coverage before your visit, free of charge.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

You can start with the $99 new patient special, then join our in-house membership plan for routine care. Larger treatment can be split into monthly payments, subject to approval.

What's included in the new patient special?

A comprehensive exam, a full set of digital x-rays and a standard cleaning. If you need a deep cleaning or other treatment, we'll explain why and give you the price before anything starts.

Do you see kids? From what age?

Yes, from their first tooth or first birthday, whichever comes first. Parents can stay in the room, and we book siblings back to back to save you a trip.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe tooth pain, swelling, a broken or knocked-out tooth, or a lost filling or crown. Call us right away. For a knocked-out tooth, keep it in milk and come in as fast as you can.

Let's get you on the schedule.

Tell us a little about what you need and we'll text you back with a time. Most new patients are seen within the week.
