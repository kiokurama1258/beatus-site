03 Our kitchen

If it wasn't swimming this morning, it won't be on the grill tonight.

Harbor & Vine started with one simple rule: cook what's fresh and local, and cook it over real fire. Every morning our kitchen team meets the boats, picks the best of the catch and builds the day's specials around it.

Vegetables come from small farms about an hour away. Pasta is rolled by hand before lunch. Our key lime pie has not changed since the day we opened, and we would like to keep it that way.

The wine list leans toward small producers, with plenty to try by the glass. Not sure what you want? Ask your server for a taste before you commit. Nobody here will rush you.

08 Good to know

Questions we hear a lot.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and someone from the floor team will answer, usually within minutes during service.

Is ordering direct really cheaper than the delivery apps?

Yes. When you order direct you pay the same prices as the dining room, there's no service fee, and delivery is a flat $3.99 within 3 miles. Apps usually mark up the menu and add their own fees, so the same order tends to cost more there.

How far do you deliver, and how long does it take?

We deliver within 3 miles of the restaurant with our own drivers. Most orders arrive in 35 to 45 minutes. On busy Friday nights we'll give you the real time when we confirm your order.

Do you take walk-ins?

Always at the bar, and on the patio when there's room. For Friday and Saturday dinner, a reservation is the safest bet.

Can the kitchen handle allergies and gluten-free requests?

Yes. Many dishes are naturally gluten-free and marked GF on the menu. Tell us about any allergy when you order or book, and the kitchen will walk you through safe options.

Do you host private events?

Our private room seats up to 60 for rehearsal dinners, birthdays and team nights. Send us the date and headcount and we'll put together a menu and a quote.
