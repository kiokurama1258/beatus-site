FAQ

Before you book.

Still unsure? That's what the free consultation is for. Bring every question.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Thirty minutes with a licensed provider, a written plan with prices, and no obligation to book anything. If you decide to treat the same day, we'll make time for it.

Will I look “done”?

Our style is conservative on purpose. We start with less, check in at two weeks and adjust only if you want more. Most people will notice you look rested, not that you had something done.

How long do Botox results last?

For most people, about three to four months. It depends on the area, the dose and your own metabolism, and we'll talk through what to expect for you.

Is laser hair removal safe for my skin tone?

We use laser settings suited to a wide range of skin tones and always do a patch test at your consultation before the first full session.

Can I cancel the membership?

Yes, any time after the first three months, with no cancellation fee. Any credit you've built up stays available for a year.
