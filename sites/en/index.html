Do you build on WordPress and Elementor?

Yes. We also build in custom HTML, which is usually lighter and faster. We will recommend the best option for your case.

How much does it cost?

A landing page is $200 flat. A multi-page website is quoted based on its size. The price is agreed before we start.

Are hosting and domain included?

No. You pay the provider you choose directly. We tell you where to sign up and set everything up.

Do I need copy and photos ready?

No. We write the copy with you and can use the photos from your Instagram.

How long does it take?

We agree on the timeline before we start, based on the size of the page.

Can I see the page before it goes live?

Yes. You get a working preview and can ask for changes.

Is the page ready for ads?

Yes, with the Meta Pixel, the Google tag and contact events installed.

Do you work with businesses in the US?

Yes. We work remotely and in English, mostly over WhatsApp and email.

Does it work for an online store?

No. E-commerce needs a different setup, and we can point you in the right direction.

How do I pay?

We agree on it with you over WhatsApp or email.
